EarthRender Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

EarthRender is a personal video production tool. It runs only on its owner's own computers and is used to make videos for the EarthRender YouTube and TikTok channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What EarthRender accesses

When the owner signs in, EarthRender receives permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to send videos to the owner's own TikTok inbox as drafts. It reads only the basic account details needed to show which account is connected (the YouTube channel name or the TikTok display name).

What EarthRender stores

The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file that is never shared or uploaded anywhere else. EarthRender does not collect, sell or share personal information, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not access anyone else's account or data.

YouTube API Services

EarthRender uses YouTube API Services. By using EarthRender, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove EarthRender's access to a Google account at any time on Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

TikTok

EarthRender uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API to send drafts to the owner's TikTok inbox. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services.

Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in EarthRender deletes its token from the computer. Removing access in the Google or TikTok settings above does the same on their side.
